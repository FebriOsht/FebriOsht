### 🚀 About me

I'm **Febriansyah**, a full-stack developer from **Lampung, Indonesia**, with a Bachelor's degree in Informatics Engineering (S.Kom). I build web applications and business systems with **Next.js, TypeScript and PostgreSQL**, and I use AI models such as Claude and Gemini every day as part of how I design, write and review code.

- 🏢 I run **[Oshtore](https://oshtore.id)**, a software agency for websites, business systems and automation.
- 🦊 I build products and free tools under **[Vulpfest](https://www.vulpfest.com)**.
- 🧰 I share small open-source tools on **[Vulpfest Lab](https://lab.vulpfest.com)**.
- 🤝 Open to collaboration on web, automation and AI projects.
